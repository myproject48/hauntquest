# Gameplay guide

## Spoiler-free start

Choose **Begin Quest** and read or skip the introduction. Explore each illustrated
room by tapping its objects, or use the room's accessible object list. Read clues
before opening an apparatus. Collected items can be inspected and used on compatible
objects from inventory. Puzzle answers are submitted with **Check**; no puzzle
solves merely by opening it. Wrong submissions explain what to reconsider and do
not remove critical items. The journal preserves discoveries and conversations.

The map shows visited rooms and available exits. Locked exits explain their
prerequisites. The Whispering Guide's subtle, guided and direct hints consider only
what you have discovered. All help is local and authored; no internet is needed.
Sound is optional. Text and visual clues cover every pitch/frequency challenge.

Progress saves after actions, including unfinished puzzle arrangements. Continue
resumes the active journey. Completing a puzzle preserves its solved appearance
and never duplicates rewards. New Game requires confirmation before replacing an
existing journey; there are no separate manual slots or cloud saves.

## Complete walkthrough — spoilers

This route completes all sixteen puzzles and gathers the evidence required for
all three final choices. For guardianship, follow the compassionate dialogue choices
below. Explore optional room objects for additional context. Use the journal's
Story tab to resume a conversation if you leave it unfinished.

### 1. Moonlit Entrance Hall

1. Examine **Portrait of Ottoline** to take the brass winding key. Inspect Wren's
   letter in inventory for the moon order and the remembered time.
2. Open **Silver candelabra**. Submit **Crescent → Half → Full → Waning** for
   *Candle Moons*. The hidden door opens.
3. Use the brass winding key on the **Dormant automaton** to solve *Wake the
   Caretaker*. Tallow gives you his lantern. Thank him warmly, then **Listen to his
   memory**. Advance through the result text to finish the linked conversation.
4. Solve **The Symbol Seal**: the displayed alphabet decodes the symbols to
   **LILAC**. Both moon symbols represent the same letter.
5. Solve **The Silver Mirror**. On the right-hand 3×3 grid light **top-right,
   center, bottom-center, bottom-right**. The mirror reverses columns, not rows.
6. Take the **Hidden door** to the library.

### 2. Forgotten Library

1. Examine the **Reading desk** to collect Ottoline's journal. Inspect it for the
   shift rule, drive-gear settings and part of the final pillar order.
2. Open the **Whispering shelf**. Shift `OLVWHQ` three letters backward and enter
   **LISTEN**. Collect the first torn recipe page.
3. Solve **The Missing Runes** by entering only the gaps: **II → IV → VI**.
4. Solve **The Evidence Board**: **Arrival → Sabotage → Alarm → Disappearance**.
5. Examine the **Cold hearth** to collect the other torn page and spare cog.
   Combine the two torn pages for *Torn Recipe*, producing silver solvent.
6. Examine the **Investor's ledger** for the sabotage evidence.
7. Go through the workshop exit. Keep the journal and cog for later apparatuses.

### 3. Alchemist's Workshop

1. Use the silver solvent on the **Corroded cabinet** for *Corroded Cabinet*.
   Take the wax cylinder.
2. Solve **Moonwater Alchemy** in this order: **Moonwater → Silver salt → Ember
   moss**. Leave grave ash out. Bench supplies are unlimited; mistakes consume
   no inventory.
3. Use the wax cylinder on the **Brass phonograph**. Hear/read the recording;
   simply inspecting an unplayed phonograph does not reveal it. It gives **73**
   and “water wakes first.”
4. Answer the Echo **“A friend. I'm listening.”** Then choose **Promise to listen
   without owning it**. Finish both conversation nodes.
5. Open the **Resonance tuner**, set it to **73**, then submit. Take the stairs
   to the clocktower.

### 4. Clocktower Chamber

1. Open the **Great clock face** and set *Stopped Hands* to **11:40** (twenty
   to midnight). Collect the resonance core.
2. Solve **Constellation Discs**, in Owl/Hare/Stag order: **1 / 3 / 2**
   (east / west / south). The chart numbers north=0, east=1, south=2, west=3.
3. Examine the **Keeper's logbook**. It supplies the final wind clue and starts
   Maul's conversation. **Refuse** his bargain, then **Reject ownership of the
   Echo**. These choices preserve the guardianship route.
4. Open the **Drive gear frame** with the spare cog available. Submit gear
   settings **2 / 0 / 3**. The cog is consumed once and the vault drawbridge opens.
5. Cross the **Vault drawbridge**.

### 5. The Echo Vault

1. Solve **The Echo Frequencies** before restoring the engine: **220 → 440 →
   330 Hz** (low → high → middle). 550 Hz is the decoy. The written plate and
   visual pulses are sufficient when muted.
2. Open **The Resonance Engine** with the resonance core. Submit **Tide → Ember
   → Gale**. The order comes from the recording, journal and logbook; Stone is
   the decoy. This restores the engine and starts the final conversation.
3. Choose an available fate, advance its result, and select **See what follows**.
   Watch or skip the illustrated reveal and continue to **The End**.

## The three endings

| Ending | Requirements and reliable route |
| --- | --- |
| **Release the Echo** | Restore the engine and choose Release. This choice is always available at the final decision, regardless of earlier attitude. |
| **Seal the Echo** | Restore the engine and either solve Moonwater Alchemy **and** Constellation Discs, or learn Maul's binding words. The walkthrough's research route enables Seal without accepting ownership. To learn his words instead, accept his bargain or confront him about sabotage, then choose Learn the binding words. |
| **Become the Echo's Guardian** | Restore the engine; obtain at least four truth fragments and two mercy points; have no accepted bargain; make the promise to listen; solve the Evidence Board and Echo Frequencies. The complete compassionate route above satisfies these conditions. |

Disabled final choices explain what is missing. Final decisions commit once and
cannot be replayed within that journey. To experience another ending, confirm
New Game and play a new journey. Continue reopens the ending already earned.
Developer review tools are not present in the standard release APK and are not
needed for any ending.

| Stopped Hands | 11:40 | Wren's letter |

| Constellation Discs | Owl=1, Hare=3, Stag=2 | Numbered compass/star chart |
| The Missing Runes | II, IV, VI | Counting tablet |
| Moonwater Alchemy | Moonwater, silver salt, ember moss | Recipe at the retort |
| The Silver Mirror | Horizontal reflection of the shown grid | Fixed pattern and mirror instruction |
| The Evidence Board | Arrival, sabotage, alarm, disappearance | Four causally linked records |
| The Echo Frequencies | 220, 440, 330 | Tuning plate, written call and pulses |

If a puzzle reports a missing prerequisite, follow its message or the guided hint.
Do not start New Game to resolve an ordinary wrong answer. You can revisit rooms
and inspect clues without losing progress. Consumed clue items remain recorded in
the journal, and critical rewards cannot be collected twice.
